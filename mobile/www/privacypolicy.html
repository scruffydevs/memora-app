Memora privacy policy

Memora is a hackathon prototype that helps carers of people with early-stage memory loss. This page explains how the Android app uses Health Connect data.

What the app reads

Only when you tap Sync now, the app reads from Health Connect: sleep, steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen and active calories for the last 14 days. It reads nothing in the background and writes nothing to Health Connect.

Where it goes

The numbers are summarised into one row per day and sent over HTTPS to the Memora server, to the profile of the person you are signed in to help. They are shown to the Memora team and to the carers connected to that person.

Your control

Watch data is switched off by default for each person. The Memora team switches it on only with consent, and switching it off deletes all watch data held for that person. You can remove the app's access at any time in Health Connect settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell the data, show adverts, or use it to train any model. This prototype is for demonstration with volunteers' own data and synthetic data, not for real patients.
